PCB Editor

Introduction to the KiCad PCB Editor

The PCB Editor is used to design printed circuit boards & footprints.

Zones

Copper zones are filled areas connected to a net. Press B to refill all zones. A zone fill respects the clearance of every item.

Zone priority

When zones overlap, the zone with the higher priority is filled first.

Design rule checking

The design rule checker (DRC) reports clearance violations, unconnected items and courtyard overlaps.
